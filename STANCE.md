# Niji stance (frozen)

**Own Your Vibe.** The room WhatsApp cannot be: no forwards, no uncle, no screenshot-as-default, no blue-tick caste, no algorithm training you to perform.

This file is the product. Taste work is done. The next work is a message that leaves one phone and arrives on another — not another tab, not another colour.

## What it is

- Privacy without a padlock ribbon. Do the crypto. Do not perform “Encrypted.”
- Paper and ink. One paper `#EBEBEC`, cards `#F7F7F8`. No gold. Silver is Namaskaram on **Vybe only**.
- Saffron `#FF9933` is an unread pip (Chat tab, Vybe Notes). It is not a brand wash.
- Hold / Pass / Fade. No streaks. No public like-count as status.
- A vybe can be a line on its own, like a note. Comments stack; a reply sits under the line it answers. The enter-key icon sits on that comment’s line when it has replies — not next to the comment mark. The reply field sits above the keyboard.
- Chat status lives on the Chat tab rail. **You** is first — tap the + on your face. A chat-row or thread face opens the chat or the profile, not a status. Vybe status stays on Vybe. The two lanes do not mix.
- A vybe tile stays short. Comments open only after the comment mark — the stack lives in that sheet, not on the tile.
- Chat `+` is one door: new 1:1 or New group as a row on that screen. No second `+` on contacts.
- A status paper keeps the face top-left. The line sits in the centre — on a mood wash or on a still.
- Namaskaram is silver, not a blue tick, not 🙏.
- Mist is a **temp ID**, not a legal name: preferred first/last plus a number we add (`priya-482`). The QR is `niji:///mist/{token}`. Scan opens the live mist. It fades when the time is up.
- Two mountain papers, same face: one **real** Niji ID (`niji:///id/{id}`) on You and inside Vybe Edit profile; one **unreal** mist paper that only lives on the Mist tab. Shop paper is the same mountain with **shop name + the seller’s Niji ID**, payload `niji:///hub/{shopId}`.
- Chat has a QR (`niji:///chat/{nijiId}`). Scan opens that trusted thread.
- Ghost / Around / Quiet are felt, not labelled as surveillance.
- Chat copy is allowed. Chat forward is not. A pass, if it ever exists, is one person and never from Ash.
- Ash, a seal, and Mist: block a capture where the phone allows; a capture that still lands tells the other side. Never promise the glass cannot leak.
- Niji ID and the mountain paper are the invite. The share photo is a picture; the QR and the caption are the door.
- Later: when a parked line leaves, it **also sits in that chat**. Later keeps a “Sent · in chat” row (and Memory if they asked).
- Hub is a stall paper, not a mall. Stall save stays on Hub.
  - One person may host **many stalls**.
  - Papers (FSSAI, GST, shop licence, MSME, cosmetic) carry a number and expiry. Verify copies the number (no official permalink) and opens the public Indian portal. Niji does not scrape the portal, store the PDF, or tick the licence as verified.
  - Expiry is typed as digits and cut as day / month / year.
  - A hosted stall can be deleted. The book, menu, and papers go with it. Stay on Hub.
  - Rank is from customer feedback, comments, follows, visits, and blocks — not an ad bid.
  - A stall can be shared: mountain paper QR (`niji:///hub/{shopId}`) and a card into chat.
  - The book on a stall: visits, enquiries, orders, sales, followers, rating.
- Calls: long-press Hide or Delete.
- Mist history is swipe-down or History. Share the temp paper from the Mist tab after mint.
- Vybe: tap a face or name to open that account. Long-press a vybe to Report (theirs) or Archive / Delete (yours). A report is private.
- AI is out.

## What we will not add

- Gold, AI, streaks, a verify tick, public For You, “Encrypted” chrome
- L-traces, hold-to-skip, a breathing launch icon
- Lock / clock / calendar chrome on the chat list
- Main bottom tab SVG changes, or a new `IconHub`
- `expo-local-authentication` in `mobile/app.json` plugins
- A second component library
- Explore of strangers before a trusted graph is real
- Hub, calls, and Vybe Explore in the same launch screenshot
- Scraping FSSAI / GST / civic portals, or a Niji “verified shop” badge that pretends to be the government

## Tabs (do not reshuffle)

Calls → Chat → Vybe → Hub → You. Land on Vybe.

Inner Chat: Trusted → Hub chat → Mist → Later. Default Trusted.

Inner Vybe: house (feed), compass (explore), four-point spark (Notes), rings (Vybe chat), person (account).

Inner tab bars have no underline. Main bar has no pip hairline.
